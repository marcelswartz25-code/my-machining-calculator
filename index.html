Before cutting

Grade descriptions are based on manufacturer catalog/product information, but the calculator's cutting speeds remain conservative starter estimates—not copied, guaranteed maker cutting data. Exact insert code, chipbreaker, workpiece grade, rigidity and machine limits matter. Aluminium needs a sharp aluminium-suitable edge; a steel chipbreaker may rub or build up.

RPM is calculated using N = (1000 × cutting speed) ÷ (π × diameter). For facing, use a lower speed near the centre unless your machine has a suitable constant-surface-speed system.
